What every engagement includes

Seven programmes.
One connected system.

Regardless of which option you choose, these are delivered as part of every PeopleEconomics® engagement.

🎯
Strategic Hiring Program

A structured, values-driven recruitment system. 8-step hiring workflow, must-have values, trial process, interview scorecard, and manager training. Every hiring decision runs from the same playbook, every time.

🧭
Value Integration Program

Translates values from words on a wall into observable, measurable behaviours. Leadership alignment, purpose co-creation, behavioural translation workshops, and a rollout plan that sticks.

🔄
360 Value Assessment

Structured feedback showing every leader how they are showing up against the values — from peers and direct reports. Removes blind spots. Builds the self-awareness that changes behaviour.

📊
Performance and Morale Matrix

A clear picture of where every person sits across two dimensions: performance and morale. Identifies who needs support, who is ready to grow, and who is heading for the door. Replaces gut feel with data.

📅
Employee Lifecycle Check-In

Seven questions at 7, 30, and 90 days of employment. Catches disengagement before it sets. Converts the probation period from an administrative exercise into a genuine development tool.

📡
Pulse Measurement (eNPS)

Quarterly Employee Net Promoter Score surveys give real-time visibility into morale across teams. Replaces guesswork with a living picture of how people are actually feeling.

📝
PE® Language Integration

PeopleEconomics® language embedded across all documentation and delivery — Position Descriptions, onboarding materials, performance tools, and every component of programme delivery. Language shapes belief. This is where the shift becomes visible.

A national call centre was running on a 47-point QA scorecard. Morale was poor. Turnover above 40%. Customer satisfaction declining despite increasing investment in training. Leadership's response had been to add more points to the scorecard.

PeopleEconomics® replaced the scorecard with two principles: give a shit, and cut the shit. Agents stopped performing and started being themselves. Call quality improved because the people making the calls felt trusted to use their judgement.

I was sceptical when they told me we were scrapping the QA scorecard. But within three months, our call quality was the best it had ever been. The agents stopped trying to pass a test and started actually caring about the people on the other end of the phone.
